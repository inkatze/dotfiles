- 2026-08-06 [dotfiles] The repo now carries four hand-written verification scripts under scripts/ -- gitleaks-rules-test.sh, ssh-lan-config-sync-test.sh, services-declaration-test.sh, and (added by specs/dev-services Task 3) postgresql-access-test.sh -- and not one of them is invoked by lefthook or by any CI job. Each says "run manually" in its own header, which is honest, and each was written precisely because the property it checks is one review keeps missing. The result is a growing body of assertions whose regressions nobody is told about: the scripts are green the day they are written and unobserved every day after. Two of them are cheap and hermetic enough to run in pre-commit today (gitleaks-rules-test.sh, services-declaration-test.sh, both a second or two, no network, no root); postgresql-access-test.sh deliberately needs a provisioned server and belongs in a CI job rather than a hook. Noting the pattern rather than a single script, since the fourth instance is what makes it one.
