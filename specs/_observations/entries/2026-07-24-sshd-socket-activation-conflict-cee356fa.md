- 2026-07-24 [dotfiles] Ubuntu 26.04 ships OpenSSH socket-activated: ssh.socket is enabled+active and ssh.service is disabled+inactive (port 22 still listens, spawned per-connection). roles/linux/tasks/ssh-server.yml asserts service name=ssh enabled=true state=started, and the Restart sshd handler restarts the same unit. Enabling ssh.service alongside an enabled ssh.socket is the known Ubuntu conflict and is a likely linux-migration Task 7 first-run failure. Decide the posture deliberately (socket activation vs classic service) and make the role assert that one; a socket-activated host also changes what Task 8 dropbear and Task 10 headless-boot verification observe.
