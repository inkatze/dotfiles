- 2026-10-04 [dotfiles] A metered hosted PR-review bot was re-triggered with its full-review comment on every new head of several large PRs; its docs say a full review re-counts the whole diff while an incremental trigger counts only changes since its last completed review, so the workspace allowance ran out and the bot answered with a trial review-limit refusal on a public repo whose plan should have been free. Lesson: re-reviews must be incremental (full only for a PR's first pass) and a quota refusal is a named stop, never silence or a retry; /bot-review now reads optional full_review_comment, rereview_comment and quota_refusal_regex for this. Open question for the operator to settle with the vendor: why the public repo was billed against a trial allowance rather than the free plan.
