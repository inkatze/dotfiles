- 2026-08-06 [dotfiles] skill-drift(execute-task): the halt protocol says to record a parked unit as a committed reference bullet '**Task <id>** - <reason>', but does not restate spec-format's constraint that a task may carry at most one reference bullet across Awaiting input, Deferred and Out of scope. A single run can reach the halt protocol more than once -- dev-services Task 3 did, once for Done-when clauses it could not verify and again for a hard-disqualifier-zone finding surfaced during convergence -- and writing the second bullet produces a spec-validate error ('named by more than one reference bullet') after the content is already written. The doctrine is clear; the skill is what a worker follows at that moment, and it reads as though each halt writes its own bullet. The fix is one clause: a task already carrying a bullet gets its existing one extended, not a second added.
