- 2026-07-24 [dotfiles] roles/linux/defaults/main.yml linux_apt_packages omits gh (GitHub CLI). The planwright pipeline requires gh for PR evidence in the state derivation and for /execute-task to open draft PRs, so orchestrate cannot complete a unit on the Linux host until gh is installed and authenticated. The macOS side gets gh via Brewfile. Consider adding gh to the Linux apt baseline.
